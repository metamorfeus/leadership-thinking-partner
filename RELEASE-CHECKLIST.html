3. Risks Peter must accept to release (in his own words)

  • Weekly Review. It ranked callers by sign-ups after pushback and ended without the review in a new test. Either apply candidate G (rule 9) and run the one check, or accept this limit for reviewed early use.
  • A person must read every document. Nobody has tested whether people catch a dropped plan part, a changed phrase or a claim credited to them. The guide says what to compare.
  • An executive-ordered claim goes into a document as stated, with the doubt in the notes only. Keep it, or also flag it inside the document.
  • When asked for a different document, the tool may never write the mode's document (two cases).
  • Claude testing Claude, with a simulated executive. No real executive, no other model, no real app until the live check.
  • Praise, engineering words and reworded phrases still occur.
  • The optional assumption check is often left out after twelve messages (Peter chose to keep twelve).
  • Free accounts: Anthropic's help pages disagree on whether they can set project instructions.
