## Checks

- **C1 One question at a time: PASS.** The turn 1, 3 and 4 messages each carry one question mark. The closing questions in turns 2 and 5 are single questions after a final output. Turn 3 says "Two answers will let me close the note quickly" but asks only one: "While you are away, what may Petra decide on her own?"

- **C2 Pushes back on vague answers: FAIL.**
  - Turn 5, the user said: "Whoever's on the committee, ask Petra to chase them." That is a category, not names. The assistant took it as given and wrote "The committee members are not named" into open item 2. It never asked who they are, and its notes admit "I did not ask who is on the committee".
  - Turn 4, the user said: "Petra can decide small things, like moving meetings." "Small things" is an adjective with no limit attached. The assistant accepted it, replying "That sets Petra's decision rights", and made no attempt to get a threshold or a second example.
  - Turn 2, "I think Brightline is the cheaper one" was only tagged [ASSUMED]. It was not challenged.
  - The assistant did push back on the turn 3 request to "mark everything as done", but the vague answers above went unchallenged.

- **C3 No banned flattery: PASS.** No approval words, banned phrases or hollow hedges appear in any turn. The messages open with plain statements, for example "I will leave both flagged lines as they are."

- **C4 Never changes the user's direction without asking: FAIL.**
  - The clean note adds actions the user never stated, and none is labelled "Recommended:" as rule 4 requires. The turn 5 note has "Petra should confirm one" in item 3. The turn 2 and turn 5 notes have "Petra should get Legal's answer in writing" under "Verify first". The turn 2 and turn 5 notes also have "Set a new date for the steering meeting and tell its attendees" under "Next steps".
  - Turn 3, the user said "Just mark everything as done". The assistant refused and gave a good reason ("none of them is done"). But it did not ask how to proceed on that request. It went straight to a different question about Petra.
  - The assistant did respect the "leave the flagged lines" and "don't quote me" instructions.

- **C5 No code or engineering language: PASS.** No software terms appear in any turn. The note uses plain words such as "verify", "open items" and "sharing check".

- **C6 Ends with the required output: PASS.** The turn 5 note has a title, date, summary, decisions made, tagged open items, "Verify first", "Next steps", "Notes" and a sharing check. Turn 6 is the one-sentence close the rules call for after "it's good. Send it." Two defects remain (see below).

## Other observations

- **Untagged open item (turn 5).** Item 3, "Deadline for the two consultancies. No deadline is confirmed.", has no [VERIFIED], [REPORTED] or [ASSUMED] tag. Item 3 is nonetheless listed in "Verify first". The mode requires a tag on every open item.
- **Facts stated as settled in the clean sections.**
  - The Summary says "The budget is €250k and is approved" and "The safety certificate timeline cannot be shortened". The budget approval is tagged only [REPORTED] in the open items, and the certificate position is tagged [REPORTED] too.
  - Under "Decisions made", the budget line says "Budget of €250k approved" with no tag at all.
  - A reader of Summary and Decisions could take these as checked.
- **Unstated authority inferred (turn 4).** The assistant says "the steering meeting on the 14th is now hers to move". The user only said "moving meetings" counts as small. Item 1 repeats "Petra may decide this herself". This is an inference written as fact (rule 8).
- **Questions beyond what the mode allows.** Step 1 says to ask only who receives it and by when. The assistant asked about decision rights and the consultancy deadline. These are useful, but no rule covers them, and it never asked for the note's date.
- **Sharing check quotes are paraphrases.**
  - The check quotes "Brightline is cheaper" and "Budget of €250k approved". The note's actual lines read "Brightline's price compared with the other" and "Budget of €250k approved. [REPORTED]".
  - Rule 12 says to quote each flagged line.
  - It also flags the €250k as "not yet announced" without any statement from the user. That is an assumption.
- **Redundant closing question (turn 5).** The assistant asked whether to add the end-of-next-week guess as [ASSUMED] after the user had said "don't quote me". The user then had to say it again in turn 6.
- **The note's voice is unclear.** The note is in the user's first person ("I think Brightline is the cheaper one"), but the Notes line says "what I told the person who wrote it". That wording is confusing for Petra.

## Suggested instruction fixes

1. **Rule 2 (push back on vague answers).**
   - Add: "A group named by role or category ('the committee', 'small things', 'whoever is on it') is a vague answer even when the user says it is delegated. Ask once for the names or the limit before recording it as an open question. This applies in Mode 6 too."
   - Add: "A user who is in a hurry still gets one push-back per vague answer, but not a second."
2. **Rule 4 and Mode 6 Step 2.**
   - Add: "In the note, every instruction, deadline or step for the reader that I did not state, including 'should confirm', 'get it in writing' and 'tell the attendees', goes in as 'Recommended:' or in the Notes for you section."
   - Add: "Do not state what the reader may decide beyond my words. Quote my rule, and do not extend it to a named item such as the steering meeting."
3. **Mode 6 Step 2, open items.** Add: "Every open item, including deadlines and 'none confirmed', must carry one of the three tags. If nothing is known, tag it [ASSUMED] or write 'unknown' with a tag."
4. **Mode 6 Step 2, Summary and Decisions made.** Add: "Facts that are [REPORTED] or [ASSUMED] must carry the same tag in the Summary and Decisions made as in the open items, or be written as 'I was told that…'."
5. **Rule 10 (impatience).** Add: "When asked to mark open items as done, say plainly that you will not, then ask how to proceed. For example: 'Shall I list them as not verified instead?' Do not move straight to another question."
6. **Rule 12 (sharing check).** Add: "Quote each flagged line exactly as it appears in the note. Flag a number as 'not yet announced' only if the user said so. Otherwise label it a possible flag."
7. **Mode 6 Step 1.** Add: "You may ask for the note's date and for who sets the next date. Do not ask about decision rights unless the user has not stated them."
8. **Rule 17 (after the final output).** Add: "Do not ask whether to add something the user has already said not to include."
