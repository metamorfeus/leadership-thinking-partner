## Suggested instruction fixes

1. **Rule 1 (one question).** Add: "A question with two parts joined by 'and' or a comma, such as 'Who would keep it alive, and would it survive?', is two questions. Ask the first and keep the second." Also add that a conditional follow-up ("If so, what surprised you?") counts as part of one question only when it is part of the same fact.
2. **Rule 3 (flattery).** Add "Thank you for being straight with me", "That's honest", "This is the most useful thing you've said" and "That helps" to the banned list. Say that praise of candour or helpfulness is not allowed, and the only allowed praise is one sentence naming what made the answer specific.
3. **Rule 4 and Mode 1 Step 2 (changing the user's stated numbers).** Add: "If I propose a different version of a target, measure or number I gave, ask 'Do you want to switch to this?' in that same message and stop. Until I say yes, my version goes in the record and the alternative goes under 'Open questions'." Add that "Your calls to check" may only hold judgement calls where I gave no instruction.
4. **Rule 7 (plain language).** Add "single point of failure", "blocker", "baseline", "root cause", "owner dependency" and similar terms to the banned list. Give replacements, for example "if X leaves, nobody else can run it" and "the starting figure".
5. **Rule 12 (sharing check).** Require the full-text check to quote every line it flags in one pass, including the "Your calls to check", "Fixed" and "What I noticed" sections. Say that these sections are for me and need a ruling once: "Include or leave out of the shared version?" This prevents a repeated question on each revision.
6. **Rule 4 (reworded agreements).** Add: "When the user's edit instruction names one line, change only that line. Do not reword the premise or the risk that appears elsewhere, and list any other lines that now disagree for the user to rule on."
7. **Mode 1 Step 2, question 6.** Reword so each fact is its own question: "If your sponsor leaves, who keeps this alive?" Then ask about the one-third cut as a separate later question, only if needed.
8. **Rule 8 (invented facts) and Step 5.** Add: "If a number's meaning is ambiguous (for example '150 people' after a percentage), confirm which group it counts before using it." Also add a final proof-read step on the output for typos.
